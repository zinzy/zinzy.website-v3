Zinzy's website / Posts / HTML Day 2026, Amsterdam edition

HTML Day is an annual celebration of HTML during which we gather in-person to write HTML, learn with and from each other, and enjoy the beautiful fusion of the real world and the independent web. HTML energy is all around us. Let’s celebrate it! Beginners are very, very welcome.

Details

  • Date: August 8, 2026
  • Time: 14:00
  • Location: Internet Archive Europe on Oudeschans 16, Amsterdam

What to bring

  • Something to write HTML on (a laptop, tablet, smartphone or paper)
  • A snack and drink for yourself

What we’ll do

  • Talk about websites
  • Write HTML
  • Share what we’ve made

How to join

Sign up on Luma!
