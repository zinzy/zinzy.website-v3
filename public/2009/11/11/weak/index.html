Weak

Nov 11, 2009
I published this piece quite a while ago. Though I enjoy the art of public record-keeping, you should know it may no longer reflect my views.

What is the deal with people and coffee? No, let’s try that again. What is the deal with people and dish water? Coffee1 these days. Is. Disgusting.

A Starbucks recently opened in Utrecht, and to celebrate the occassion, a dear friend of mine, whom I hadn’t seen in two years, suggested we meet there for a pumpkin spiced whatever.

Pat, I’d love to buy a middle finger. Or else perhaps just a question mark.
