After 15 years of silence, Merlin Mann's hit blog 43Folders has gone offline. His last post dates from October 2011 and discusses [the release of Instapaper 4 for iOS](https://web.archive.org/web/20260310011448/https://marco.org/2011/10/17/instapaper-4-released). In the early zeroes, I would visit daily, enjoying the repartee between him and the things he encountered in the world, but mostly eager for productivity tips. 

I _know_ the site because I tried to go there today, and found some generic new, contentless interface. I suppose I'm still eager for productivity tips.
