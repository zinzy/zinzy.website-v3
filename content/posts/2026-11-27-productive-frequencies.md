After 15 years of silence, Merlin Mann's hit blog 43Folders has gone offline. I don't know if it was a big happening on social media when people found out? In the early zeroes, I would visit daily, enjoying the repartee between him and the things he encountered in the world, but mostly eager for productivity tips. 

I _know_ the blog went offline because I visited it today, and found some generic new, contentless interface. I suppose I'm still eager for productivity tips.
