tl;dr: school year's off to a great start!

And just like that, the end of summer ushers in a new school year. I say "end of summer" with the totem-pro-parte enthusiasm of someone who is yearning for the rainy days of blankets, books, tea, and early nights. I know we have another month to go.

The Netherlands, much like the rest of Europe, have been so uncomfortably hot that leaves have turned brown and fell to the ground. Pair it with a week of very welcome fitful bursts of rain, and I'm ready to put the kettle on and call it a season.

After a week and a half of working from home, I was able to return to the office on Thursday, where I was met with friendly faces and warm hugs. It's a privilege to have a job this good, fun, rewarding, and challenging.

On Saturday, I danced my very first 5Rhythms wave with teacher [Fabiola at the Munganga Theater](https://www.5rhythms.com/classes/Fabwaves-289250). Anja has been a practitioner for over a dozen years now, and it was lovely to finally join her. I had attended a few ecstatic dance sessions in the past, but there's something about this specific practice that makes me feel it is going to stay with me for a very long time to come.

At Flevopark, our beloved dog-walking spot, the community hub burned down this week. The firefighters let it burn down in a controlled setting because they couldn't get enough water there in time, and ever since neighbours have lingered there around the remaining picnic table, sharing stories, commiserating in the loss of this sweet building. I hope the city will be able to help put back something even better.
