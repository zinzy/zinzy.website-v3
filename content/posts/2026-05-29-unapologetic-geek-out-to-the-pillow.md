After [listening and talking and listening and talking and listening](/2026/02/15/week-7-of-2026-funny-valentine/) it's nice to enter the red room and shut up. The pillow. You're one in a set of three dozen, lined up along the wall and in the centre, each supporting the weight of another, smaller pillow. I enter the room with my left foot, I learn during an introduction course, hold my palms together, and bow. I'm not curious to know what other people bow for, but I bow for the pillow.

One of the sweetest misconceptions that exists around meditation is that we must have a quiet mind to do it. I imagine there are forms of meditation that require this, but in the practice that speak to me, we let the mind roam freely. Thoughts come as they please and depart once they find there is no coat rack for them to rest on. 25 minutes in almost complete physical and auditory silence, then a walking meditation, and then 25 more. Just me and the pillow.
