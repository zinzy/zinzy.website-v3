HTML Day is an annual celebration of HTML during which we gather in-person to write HTML, learn with and from each other, and enjoy the beautiful fusion of the real world and the independent web. [HTML energy](https://html.energy/) is all around us. Let's celebrate it! Beginners are very, very welcome.

### Details

- Date: August 8, 2026
- Time: 14:00
- Location: somewhere in Central Amsterdam

### What to bring

- Something to write HTML on (a laptop, tablet, smartphone or paper)
- (Depending on location) A snack and drink for yourself
- (Depending on location) A picnic blanket

### What we'll do

- Talk about websites
- Write HTML
- Share what we've made
